The protagonist of the game, leaves town in search of their [[Professor Pine (MOM)|mom]] and [[Professor Pine (DAD)|dad]],  who had left on a research expedition, after they had left without the family [[rotom]], who becoems their partner pokemon.

The player chooses a class at the start of the game, giving them an affinity for a specific pokemon type as well as additional abilities in the game.

Creepy Kid - Ghost

Artsy Kid - Fire

Nature Kid - Grass

Sportsy Kid - Water

Popular Kid - Ice

Smart Kid - Flying

The class they choose also determines what form rotom takes.
