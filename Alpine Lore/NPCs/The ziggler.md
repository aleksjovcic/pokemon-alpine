#follower 
A young boy rooting around in the trash at [[the lodge]], who insists that his name is The Ziggler. his parents are staying at the lodge but he prefers to hang with his several zigzagoon

Talking to him, you can fight him. If you beat him, he'll give you something he found in the trash. What? Who knows?!?

His parents are looking for him. Once you've spoken to both him and his parents, you can either reveal his hiding spot, or keep his secret.

If you keep his secret, he's grateful, and will join you as a partner trainer. Or give you a Zigzagoon?

Partner pokemon: Several zigzagoon
