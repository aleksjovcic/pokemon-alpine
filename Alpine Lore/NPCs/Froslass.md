#follower 
A ice/ghost pokemon that haunts [[the lodge]]. It roams the halls at night and freezes trainers in their rooms. During the daytime it can be found in room 237, however the door to that room is frozen shut on the inside. 

The froslass is blamed for the majority of the hauntings in the lodge. Most of the guests assume that the froslass is malicious and is freezing the guests out of revenge, however in reality it is actually doing so to protect them from the [[Dusknoir]] that is haunting the lodge.

Based on the player's decisions, Froslass may end up joining the [[player]] in a battle against the Dusknoir, or it may battle against the player if they choose to ally with the Dusknoir. If the player allies with froslass to defeat dusknoir, it will join the player's team afterwards, no longer needing to protect the lodge.
